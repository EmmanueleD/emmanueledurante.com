# Emmanuele Durante — Visual identity

The portfolio palette draws on the blue, cool white and red shirts in the supplied reference. Use cool white #F5F5FA for the page, #E8E7F0 for alternating surfaces, dark blue #173954 for text and #32658E for the services section. Use #B81A19 for primary actions and the D silhouette. The horizontal logo bar uses shirt blue #427CAC. Keep the same geometry for the header, favicon and touch icons.
